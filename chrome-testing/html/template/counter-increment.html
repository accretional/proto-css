counter-increment

counter-increment: none
  1. No counter
  2. No counter
  3. No counter
  4. No counter
  5. No counter

none — no counter incremented

counter-increment: section
  1. First item
  2. Second item
  3. Third item
  4. Fourth item
  5. Fifth item

section — increments by 1 (default)

counter-increment: section 2
  1. Even step
  2. Even step
  3. Even step
  4. Even step
  5. Even step

section 2 — increments by 2

counter-increment: section -1
  1. Countdown
  2. Countdown
  3. Countdown
  4. Countdown
  5. Countdown

section -1 — decrements (from 6)
