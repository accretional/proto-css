Mask Border Repeat

mask-border-repeat

stretch

stretched

mask-border-repeat: stretch

Mask border slice is stretched to fill the edge region. Default value.

repeat

repeated

mask-border-repeat: repeat

Tiles are repeated to fill the edge. May be clipped at boundaries.

round

rounded

mask-border-repeat: round

Tiles are scaled so a whole number fits. No clipping occurs.

space

spaced

mask-border-repeat: space

Tiles are spaced evenly. Extra space is distributed between tiles.
