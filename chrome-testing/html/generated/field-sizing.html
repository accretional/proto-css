Field Sizing

field-sizing

Experimental property -- controls whether form fields have a fixed size or size to their content.

field-sizing: fixed ! important
<textarea>

Fixed dimensions; content scrolls if it overflows.

<input type="text">

Width stays constant regardless of text length.

<select>

Fixed width; long options may get clipped.

field-sizing: fixed ! important
<textarea>

Grows and shrinks to fit content. No scrollbar needed.

<input type="text">

Width matches the text content length.

<select>

Width adjusts to selected option text.
