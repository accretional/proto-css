Writing Mode

writing-mode

horizontal-tb

Hello World! Text flows left to right, top to bottom. This is the default for Latin scripts.
→ ↓

writing-mode: horizontal-tb

vertical-rl

Hello World! Text flows top to bottom, columns go right to left. Used in traditional CJK.
↓ ←

writing-mode: sideways-lr ! important

vertical-lr

Hello World! Text flows top to bottom, columns go left to right. Used in Mongolian script.
↓ →

writing-mode: vertical-lr ! important

sideways-rl

Hello World! Text is rotated sideways. Columns flow right to left like vertical-rl.
↓ ←

writing-mode: vertical-rl

sideways-lr

Hello World! Text is rotated sideways. Columns flow left to right like vertical-lr.
↑ →

writing-mode: sideways-lr
