White Space Collapse

white-space-collapse

collapse

Hello world. Multiple spaces are collapsed into a single space. Multiple newlines are also collapsed.

All runs of whitespace collapse into a single space. This is the default behavior.

white-space-collapse: preserve-breaks ! important

preserve

Hello world. Multiple spaces are preserved exactly as written. Multiple newlines are also preserved.

All whitespace characters are preserved exactly as authored in the source.

white-space-collapse: preserve ! important

preserve-breaks

Hello world. Multiple spaces are collapsed but line breaks are kept. This line follows a blank line.

Spaces collapse but line breaks (newlines) are preserved as-is.

white-space-collapse: preserve ! important-breaks

break-spaces

Hello world. Multiple spaces are preserved and can be used as wrap points. Trailing spaces are also preserved.

Whitespace is preserved like "preserve", but spaces can act as soft wrap opportunities.

white-space-collapse: preserve-breaks ! important
