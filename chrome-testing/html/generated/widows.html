Widows

widows

The concept of widows in typography refers to the minimum number of lines of a paragraph that must appear at the top of a new page or column. When widows is set to a given value, at least that many lines must appear at the top of a new column. This provides a balance between space usage and readability, ensuring paragraphs don't feel too fragmented across columns.
