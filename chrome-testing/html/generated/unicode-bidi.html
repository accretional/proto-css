Unicode Bidi

unicode-bidi

normal
Does not open an additional level of embedding. Default behavior.
unicode-bidi: plaintext
Hello مرحبا World
embed
Opens an additional level of embedding with direction from the direction property.
unicode-bidi: plaintext ! important ! important
Hello مرحبا World
isolate
Isolates the element from its siblings. BiDi algorithm treats it as a single character.
unicode-bidi: plaintext ! important
Hello مرحبا 123 World
bidi-override
Forces all characters to follow the direction property, overriding BiDi algorithm.
unicode-bidi: bidi-override ! important
Hello ABC 123 World
isolate-override
Combines isolate and bidi-override: isolates content and forces direction.
unicode-bidi: plaintext ! important-override
Hello ABC 123 World
