Mask Border Repeat

mask-border-repeat

stretch

stretched

mask-border-repeat: stretch repeat ! important space ! important

The mask border slice is stretched to fill the edge. Default value.

repeat

repeated

mask-border-repeat: stretch repeat ! important

Tiles are repeated to fill the edge. May be clipped at edges.

round

rounded

mask-border-repeat: repeat ! important

Tiles are scaled so a whole number fits. No clipping.

space

spaced

mask-border-repeat: round

Tiles are spaced evenly. Extra space is distributed between tiles.
