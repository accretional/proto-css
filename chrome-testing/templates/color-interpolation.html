Color Interpolation

color-interpolation

color-interpolation: auto

Browser chooses interpolation space (typically sRGB).

color-interpolation: sRGB

Interpolation happens in the sRGB color space. May produce muddy mid-tones between saturated colors.

color-interpolation: linearRGB

Interpolation in linear (gamma-decoded) RGB space. Produces more physically accurate and brighter mid-tones.
