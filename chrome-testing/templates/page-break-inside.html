Page Break Inside

page-break-inside

auto — Cards may split across columns

Card A: Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit.
Card B: This card may be split across column boundaries by the browser.
Card C: Short card.
Card D: Another paragraph of text that fills space in the column layout.
Card E: Final content block.

page-break-inside: auto

Browser may break elements across pages/columns.

avoid — Cards stay intact

Card A: Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit.
Card B: This card will NOT be split — it stays intact across columns.
Card C: Short card.
Card D: Another paragraph of text that fills space in the column layout.
Card E: Final content block.

page-break-inside: avoid

Prevents breaking inside this element.
